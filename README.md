# D2C Creative Intelligence Engine

> Turn any D2C product URL into customer insights, strategic creative angles, AI-powered creative QA, and an Excel-ready creative testing matrix.

Built with **n8n + Google Gemini + Google Drive**.

---

## 🚀 What Is This?

Most D2C brands don't have a lack of products.

They have a lack of a **system to decide what creative ideas are actually worth testing**.

This workflow takes a product URL and turns it into a structured creative intelligence workflow:

```text
Form Trigger
     ↓
Product Page Fetch
     ↓
Product Information Extraction
     ↓
Clean & Structure Product Data
     ↓
Validate Product Data
     ↓
Customer Intelligence Analysis
     ↓
Parse Customer Analysis
     ↓
Creative Strategy Generation
     ↓
Parse Creative Strategy
     ↓
Creative Critic & QA
     ↓
Parse QA Results
     ↓
Build Creative Testing Matrix
     ↓
Convert to XLSX
     ↓
Google Drive Upload
```
